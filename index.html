About Me

The Person Behind the Edit

I'm Harjyot Sohal, a video editor and motion graphics artist with a passion for cinematic storytelling. I believe every frame is an opportunity to make someone feel something, and I obsess over the details that most people never consciously notice.

My work spans sports content, documentaries, branded campaigns, and social-first video; always with the same commitment to pacing, emotion, and visual impact. I've shipped content for major brands including FanDuel, Miller Lite, and Captain Morgan through my time at OwnersBox.

Whether it's a 60-second TikTok or a multi-camera live broadcast, I bring the same energy: intentional, fast, and built to land.
